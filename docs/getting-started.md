# Getting started

## Install

1. Download the latest zip from [GitHub releases](https://github.com/antnsn/Craftbound/releases).
2. Unzip it into `World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns`, so that you have `AddOns\Craftbound\Craftbound.toc`.
3. Start the game, or restart it if it is running. `/reload` doesn't pick up a new addon.

## Your first session

1. **Log in.** If your character has Enchanting or Alchemy, Craftbound picks that guide by itself. If it has both, type `/craft guide` and choose one.
2. **Look at the tracker.** It is a small box on the right of the screen showing your skill, the next step and how many reagents you still need. Drag it wherever you like.
3. **Go shopping.** Open the auction house. The **Craftbound shopping** panel sits next to it. Click **Scan prices**, then **Buy ...** and **Confirm** for each reagent. At a merchant, click **Buy route reagents** to get vials and rods. See [Auction house](features/auction-house.html).
4. **Open your profession window.** The Craftbound panel docks to its right, and the step's recipe is already selected. Click **Craft**, or bind a key and press it. See [Profession window](features/profession-window.html).
5. **Train when it says so.** A red line appears when you can train the next rank, and before your skill hits the cap the step becomes *Train ... at a trainer*.
6. **Stop whenever you like.** Next time you log in, Craftbound says where you were.

{: .tip }
Bind **Craft the next step** to a key: Escape > Options > Key Bindings > AddOns > Craftbound. Then levelling is one key, pressed again and again.

## What it needs from you

- **Click to buy and to craft.** The game only allows purchases and crafting from a click or a key press, and Craftbound never buys anything you haven't confirmed.
- **Open the profession window once.** Craftbound reads your recipes and their real reagents there. It also reads your skill when you log in.
- **Tick off what it can't see.** Craftbound can't tell when you have finished disenchanting, for example. Click **Done**, or shift-click the line in the route.
