## Why Craftbound exists

Taking a profession from 1 to 300 is one of the dullest jobs in the game. You read a guide in a browser and work out what to buy. You search the auction house one item at a time, find the recipe in the profession window and press Create again and again. Then the next evening you have forgotten where you were.

Craftbound does that job for you. It knows the route and what is in your bags and bank. It does the shopping in the auction house, puts the right recipe in front of you, and remembers where every character stopped.

## What it does

- **The next step, always.** One line tells you what to do next, for example *Craft Enchant Bracer - Lesser Stamina x22 (to skill 164)*, *Train Expert at a trainer* or *Make a Runed Silver Rod and keep it in your bags*. [More](features/profession-window.html)
- **In the profession window.** A panel beside it shows the step, its skill-up colour, your reagents and the whole route. It selects the recipe for you, and one button crafts what the step still needs. [More](features/profession-window.html)
- **One key.** Bind **Craft the next step** and press it. Enchants go on the right piece of gear, and the next press answers "replace the enchant?". [More](features/key-bindings.html)
- **In the auction house.** A shopping list for the rest of the route, minus what you already have. **Buy next** scans the prices and buys after you confirm the server's price. [More](features/auction-house.html)
- **At merchants.** One button buys the vials and rods the route needs. [More](features/auction-house.html#merchants)
- **Picks up where you left off.** Progress is saved per character. A tracker and a message at login tell you what comes next. [More](features/progress.html)
- **Uses real data.** Once you have opened a recipe in the profession window, Craftbound uses its real reagents instead of the guide's. [More](features/guides.html)
