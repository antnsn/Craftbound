# Key bindings

Escape > Options > Key Bindings > AddOns > **Craftbound**:

| Binding | What one press does |
| --- | --- |
| **Craft the next step** | The same as the panel's Craft button: crafts the step's batch, or puts one enchant on your gear. After an enchant on your own gear, the next press answers "replace the enchant?" (no question for an item in the **Enchant on** sidebar, or with **Overwrite my enchants** on). |
| **Auction house: buy next / confirm** | The same as the shopping panel's big button: scan, buy, confirm. |

With the profession window open, levelling Enchanting becomes one key pressed twice per enchant: once to cast it and once to replace the old one, or once with an item in the **Enchant on** sidebar. Alchemy is one press per batch.

{: .note }
A key press counts as a click to the game, which is why these work where an automatic macro wouldn't. Craftbound never crafts or buys on its own.
